# Orbit Engineering: search visibility and enquiry growth

Prepared 13 October 2026. This is a working plan for the owner and engineering team. Current website changes are local; nothing in this plan authorizes publication, account changes, outreach, advertising spend or automated submissions. The 90-day schedule starts after the owner chooses to launch the clean site and measurement is available.

## Define the lakhs target precisely

The owner's ambition is traffic in lakhs. Use **100,000 human page views over a rolling 30-day period** as a proposed long-term reporting target, then confirm the reporting period when measurement begins. This is an ambition, not a traffic forecast or a 90-day promise. No verified traffic baseline, search-volume dataset, conversion history or ranking forecast is available in this workspace.

These measures answer different questions:

| Measure | What it tells us | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page views | Pages viewed, including repeat views | Report with users and the date range; exclude internal testing and obvious automated traffic |
| Users | People/devices recognized by the selected analytics system | Reach estimate; consent, device changes and blocking affect measurement |
| Search impressions | Times the site appeared in eligible search results | Discovery opportunity; an impression is not a website visit |
| Organic clicks | Clicks from the search engine to the website | Compare Google and Bing separately; do not label them unique visitors |
| Qualified enquiries | Received enquiries matching a real service and project need | Primary business outcome; deduplicate phone, WhatsApp and email contacts |
| Quotations and awarded work | Commercial progress after qualification | Maintain with the sales team; record source only when known |

Google's reports distinguish views from users and search clicks from impressions. A larger view count can result from the same visitor opening several pages; it is not proof of wider reach. [GA4 Pages and screens](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12926732?hl=en), [Search Console performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en).

For this B2B business, a relevant plant operator, consultant, EPC contractor or procurement team member can be more valuable than many unrelated visits. Track both reach and qualified demand. Do not buy bot traffic, fabricate testimonials or publish unrelated trending content to inflate the target.

## Where growth should come from

Use the existing service and product pages as commercial destinations. Build educational content that helps the same buyers solve a real problem, with links to the relevant service, equipment category and enquiry path.

| Audience and intent | Useful content | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bhopal/MP buyer seeking a supplier | Existing water treatment, automation, installation and maintenance pages with a clear scope | Share site, process and requirement with Orbit |
| Engineer selecting a meter | Selection guide covering fluid, conductivity, pipe condition, accuracy requirement and installation | Review the relevant flow-meter category |
| Project team planning controls | PLC/SCADA scope, I/O, communication, alarms, data ownership and acceptance checklist | Request a scoped automation discussion |

The local build now connects a resources hub, four new engineering guides, four project experience pages and two working tools in this way, bringing the site from 35 to 47 public pages. Each page offers useful information before its sales call to action. Both calculators show formulas, units, examples, assumptions and limits in the initial HTML, with interactive inputs after loading. The estimates are preliminary calculations, not final system designs or promised savings.

Topic examples are editorial opportunities, not claims of measured keyword demand. Use actual Search Console/Bing queries and recurring sales questions to choose the next topics. Search for a query manually only to understand intent; one personal search result is not a dependable ranking measurement.

Google recommends original, useful information and clear sourcing/authorship. Existing repository descriptions are first-party source material, not independent project verification. For stronger experience pages, collect approved site photographs, exact contract role, commissioning records and measured outcomes. Add the real reviewer only after a real review; do not invent engineers, credentials or completion dates. ## Distribution that supports the engineering pages

After the owner authorizes publication on external channels, reuse each substantial page in formats that fit its audience:

- An engineer's LinkedIn explanation with one diagram or commissioning lesson, linking to the complete guide or relevant tool.
- A short original site walkthrough or equipment explanation, with captions and a useful description. Publish project footage only with appropriate permission.
- An approved client/supplier reference from their genuine project or partner page where a relationship exists. Request factual attribution; avoid link exchanges purchased for rankings.
- A useful contribution to a relevant industry association, event or technical publication based on actual engineering experience.
- A service reply to an existing opted-in business conversation when the recipient's problem matches the guide. Do not send unsolicited bulk messages.

Use consistent campaign parameters for external links when analytics is configured, for example `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=engineering_resources`. Link to the most relevant page. Do not append campaign parameters to internal navigation, and keep canonical URLs free of those parameters.

Scaled low-value pages, keyword stuffing, hidden text and paid links intended to manipulate rankings conflict with Google's spam policies. The useful content and distribution approach above is intended to earn relevant attention through the company's actual work. [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

## Google, Bing and AI answer discovery

Keep important content in crawlable HTML, use clear internal links and keep structured data consistent with visible facts. Google's AI features follow ordinary Search eligibility; special AI schema is not required. Inclusion and citation remain search-engine decisions. [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

Treat `/llms.txt` as an optional discovery directory. Google clarified in June 2026 that it does not change Search visibility or ranking. Google also retired FAQ rich results in May 2026; useful visible FAQs still help readers, but do not count FAQ markup as an expected rich-result win. [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

After release, use Bing's AI Performance report where available to review cited URLs and grounding queries alongside ordinary search clicks. Those counts describe citations across supported AI surfaces; they do not establish ranking position or all-AI visibility. [Bing AI Performance announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/).

Use the existing explicit IndexNow command only for genuinely added, changed or deleted public URLs after the ownership proof is live. It is a discovery notification, not a promise of crawling, indexing or ranking. Do not resubmit unchanged pages each day. [IndexNow FAQ](https://www.indexnow.org/faq).

## Measurement and decision rules

Record a dated weekly row with: reporting period; source; landing-page group; branded/non-branded query group; impressions; clicks; CTR; users; page views; enquiry-action clicks; actual qualified enquiries; quotations; and notes on changes. Use `unknown` for unavailable data, never zero. Keep Google and Bing exports identifiable and exclude preview/local hostnames from production analytics.

The current contact flow prepares a WhatsApp/email message. Clicking a contact button does **not** prove that the message was sent or received. If analytics is later enabled, label `phone_click`, `whatsapp_click` and `email_click` as intent events; have sales record received and qualified enquiries separately. Do not send names, phone numbers, email addresses or free-form enquiry text as analytics parameters. Existing CSP allows only the site's own connections, so a future analytics integration needs an explicit, tested provider configuration rather than a pasted tracking script.

| Observed pattern | Next investigation |
| --- | --- |
| Pages absent from the index | Inspect actual HTTP status, noindex/robots, canonical choice, crawl access and content value |
| Relevant impressions rise but clicks lag | Check query intent, title/description clarity and competing result formats; compare the same device/country/query group |
| Clicks grow but enquiries do not | Inspect service relevance, mobile navigation, examples, contact clarity and actual contact delivery |
| Two pages answer the same intent | Decide which page should own it; improve differentiation or consolidate with a relevant permanent redirect |
| Tools attract visits but no onward interest | Add an appropriately placed explanation and relevant service link; keep the tool useful without a form gate |
| A guide or project brings qualified enquiries | Improve supporting evidence and create the next genuinely distinct answer in that topic |

Compare complete 28-day periods to reduce weekday effects, with year-on-year context when enough history exists. Record seasonality and campaigns. Average position is contextual; examine the page/query/device segment before making changes. A small sample should not justify a traffic forecast.

Use field Core Web Vitals when the site has enough real-user data; lab checks are diagnostics rather than proof of real-world performance. Google's published good thresholds at the 75th percentile are LCP at most 2.5 seconds, INP at most 200 milliseconds and CLS at most 0.1. Check mobile and desktop separately. [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals).
